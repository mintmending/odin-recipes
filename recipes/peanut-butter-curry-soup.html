Peanut Butter Curry Soup

Recipe by PickUpLimes

Description

A combination of two delicious ingredients: peanut butter and curry.

This soup is thick and creamy thanks to the sweet potatoes, and ever so slightly sweet with the addition of a banana. And yes, you read that correctly: banana in a soup. It's so good!

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp (5ml) olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 Tbsp (8g) fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 Tbsp (6g) curry powder
  • 2 tsp (4g) ground coriander
  • 2 tsp (4.5g) sweet paprika powder
  • ⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
  • 4 cups (960ml) vegetable broth
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into 1cm cubes
  • 1 banana, cut into coins
  • 1 medium carrot, chopped
  • ½ cup (130g) natural peanut butter
  • 1⅔ cups (400ml) canned full-fat coconut milk
  • 4 slices multigrain bread, toasted

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onions, garlic, and ginger for 3 minutes. Add splashes of water as needed to deglaze the pot.
  2. Add the spices, stirring to toast for 30 - 60 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the broth, sweet potatoes, banana, carrot, and peanut butter. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, cover with a lid, and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the coconut milk, and blend using an immersion blender or standing blender. Heat through completely.
  5. Serve with bread or croutons, and enjoy!
