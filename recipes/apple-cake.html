Vegan Apple Cake with Cinnamon Crumble Topping

Recipe by Bianca Zapatka

Description

This Vegan Apple Cake with Cinnamon Streusel will be your new favorite go-to apple recipe! It's soft, moist, topped with crunchy crumbles, and super simple yet incredibly delicious. The perfect afternoon treat or dessert to serve your family or friends after a scrumptious meal.

The ingredients of this vegan apple cake are pretty easy to find. You also don't have to worry about breaking your budget since all the ingredients are inexpensive.

Ingredients

Apple Cake

  • 450g apples, chopped
  • 250g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp baking soda
  • 0.5 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 100g cane sugar
  • 120ml non-dairy milk
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 100g apple sauce
  • 120g vegan butter, melted

Cinnamon Streusel

  • 75g all-purpose flower
  • 0.5 tsp cinnamon
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 35g vegan butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Lightly grease the sides of a 9-inch springform pan (22cm) and line the bottom with parchment paper.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and sugar. Set aside.
  3. In a measuring jar, whisk together the non-dairy milk, vanilla extract, and lemon juice. Pour the milk mixture into the dry flour mixture. Add the apple sauce and melted vegan butter and stir with a whisk or spatula until just combined.
  4. Transfer the batter into your prepared pan. Spread the choppen apples on top, and gently press them into the dough.
  5. Make the streusel by mixing the flour, cinnamon, sugar, and cold vegan butter pieces into streusel. Then crumble it over your cake.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven for approx. 45-50 minutes or until a skewer inserted in the center of the cake comes out almost clean.
  7. Allow to cool for 20 minutes in the pan. Then remove onto a wire rack and allow to cool completely. Once cool and settled slice and enjoy with vegan ice cream, pecans, vegan caramel sauce, or as it is!
